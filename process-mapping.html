Overview

Why map your processes?

You cannot improve, automate or select software for a process nobody can describe. Process maps make the work visible, expose bottlenecks and duplicated effort, and resolve the confusion and conflict that come from different people doing the same job differently. We use Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN 2.0), the widely used standard notation, adapted so your team can read the maps easily.

Signs you need this

  • Different people do the same job in different ways
  • Hand-offs between departments cause delays and errors
  • You are about to select or implement new software
  • New staff take too long to learn how things work
  • You need documented processes for ISO 9001 or a customer audit

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does mapping take?

It depends on the number and size of processes. A single end-to-end process often takes one to two weeks including interviews, a workshop and the final maps.

Which tools do you use?

Any BPMN-capable tool. We can deliver in the tool you already use, or in formats such as Visio, PDF and editable diagrams.

Do you map in person or remotely?

Both. Observing the work in person gives the most accurate maps; workshops can run online.
