Overview

Where does the time in your firm actually go?

In a professional services firm, your people and their hours are the product. Yet much of that time disappears into rework, unclear handoffs, chasing approvals and work that never reaches an invoice. We work alongside your partners, managers and frontline staff to map how engagements really flow, from proposal to final billing. Together we build process measures that connect daily work to your value proposition, so everyone from the front line to the executive team can see what good looks like. The result is better delivery, higher productivity, more engaged staff and clients who notice the difference.

Signs it's time to act

  • Utilization looks healthy, but realization and margins keep slipping
  • Write-offs and write-downs are routine at month end
  • Senior people spend their days on admin and rework
  • Each team delivers the same service a different way
  • Staff are stretched, yet you hesitate to take on new work

Industry challenges

What we see in professional services.

Leakage between hours worked and hours billed

Scope creep, unbilled time and late time entry quietly erode realization. Most firms cannot see where the gap opens up.

Inconsistent delivery

Without standard work for core services, quality and turnaround depend on who is assigned, which makes estimating and pricing hard.

Capacity that is hard to see

Partners and managers rarely know true available capacity, so they either overload key people or turn away work.

Engagement and retention

Busy, unclear workdays wear people down. Losing an experienced professional costs clients, knowledge and recruiting time.

How we help

How we help

KPI framework for key processes

Digital workflow and AI support

Typical scenarios

What improvement looks like.

Illustrative examples of the kind of change we work on.

Month-end billing at a 40-person accounting firm

Before: invoices went out two to three weeks after month end while managers chased missing time entries.

After: daily time capture and a simple billing checklist let invoices go out within days of month end.

Deliverable review in an engineering consultancy

Before: reports waited in a single principal's inbox, stretching turnaround and frustrating clients.

After: tiered review criteria and delegated sign-off for routine reports shortened turnaround and freed senior time.

Client intake at a mid-sized law practice

Before: new matters were opened by email, with conflicts checks and engagement letters handled inconsistently.

After: a standard intake workflow with automated reminders made matter opening consistent and easier to track.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do you measure productivity in a firm that sells expertise?

We start with measures your partners already recognize, such as utilization, realization, turnaround time and rework, then add a small set of service-level and quality measures for key processes. Everything is tracked against your own baseline.

Will this disrupt billable work?

We plan observations and workshops around your delivery calendar and keep time away from client work to a minimum. Much of our analysis uses data you already have, such as time entries and billing reports.

Is this only for large firms?

No. We focus on small and mid-sized Canadian firms, where a few hours recovered per person each week makes a real difference to margin and workload.

Do you recommend new software?

Only when it solves a problem we have confirmed. We usually fix the process first, then look at automation or AI tools for intake, scheduling, time capture or reporting.
