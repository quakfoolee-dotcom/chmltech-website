Quak Foo Lee founded Chmltech in 2008. He is a chemical engineer with more than 15 years of experience across chemical engineering, business management and AI consulting.

He spent the first part of his career in process plants. He built and expanded a biodiesel plant, managed a pilot plant that turned biomass sugars into bio-glycols, and scaled up the production of quantum dots and graphene oxide from the lab bench to pilot scale. Along the way he ran operations, quality and safety programs, and hired and trained plant teams.

Process engineering teaches a simple habit: understand how the work flows before you change it. Quak now applies that habit to business operations. He maps how a company really works, finds where time and money are lost, and then recommends the systems, automation or AI that fix the cause.

Since 2019 he has advised Canadian small and medium-sized businesses on operational efficiency and digital technology, and he has delivered more than 300 Digital Adoption Plans. He also trains teams in Lean Six Sigma at Yellow, Green and Black Belt level.

Quak is now completing a Master of Science in AI Engineering. He writes the articles on the Insights page and builds the free courses and practice exams on this site.
