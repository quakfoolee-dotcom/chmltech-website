    How the model works
    • Orders arrive at about 1.6 a day. Each is 20 windows worth $20,000, with $8,000 of materials bought when the order is confirmed and $4,000 of labour added on the line.
    • The customer takes about 3 days to accept a quote. Order entry and supplier lead time take about 7 days. Delivery takes 2 days and is promised within 15 days of the order.
    • The line has five machines. Each needs 0.4 of a day per order and holds two orders in the buffer after it. Machines stop at random and take about 2 hours to repair.
    • Orders wait until a full batch is ready, then run together. The first machine pays one changeover per batch.
    • Cash tied up is money spent on open orders less any deposit received. The model shows won orders only and ignores weekends.
    • Each change restarts the model and runs one year in the background, so the figures you see are settled results. They are averages from a random simulation and will move a little.
