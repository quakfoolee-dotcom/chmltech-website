    Method

    How we rank and stay independent.

    How we rank

    Your problems point to software categories; your priorities and maturity gaps set the order. Products are ranked by fit to your answers, then sized to your team and budget.

    Where the data comes from

    Vendor documentation and public sources. Scores are our analyst rating: confirm them in a demo. Prices were checked in November 2026 and are in US dollars unless marked.

    Independence

    Chmltech does not resell software, takes no referral fees and accepts no paid placement. Vendors can submit product information.

    Full method
    Your business
    Industry, size and current tools filter the problems and software to what fits your type of business.
    Digital maturity
    Five functions rated Manual, Digitized, Connected or Optimized, today and in two years. The gaps show where to invest.
    Roadmap and quick win
    Categories are ranked Must, Should and Could. The quick win can go live in weeks within your budget. ERP and systems you already own are never quick wins.
    Right-sized
    Smaller firms see lighter tools; larger firms see ERP or warehouse systems.
    Capability scores
    Built in, set up without code, add-on, or not offered. Company facts are checked against a source linked in each profile.
    AI tools
    For each job, six tools (five for automation) are compared on four Canadian essentials (free plan, Microsoft 365 or Google, French, data privacy) and four job features, from vendor information checked by an independent reviewer. Other AI tools are listed, not scored.
    Vendor directory
    Listings widen a shortlist; they are not scored and never change a product's score.
