Advanced materials producer, BCIn progress

Commercial-scale front-end engineering design (FEED)

Taking a new advanced-materials process from pilot plant to a commercial-scale design, area by area, from raw-material preparation to finished product.

Scope

  • Process design basis and process flow diagrams for every plant area
  • Mass and energy balances and stream tables
  • Equipment sizing and FEED-level equipment datasheets
  • Capital and operating cost estimates to AACE Class 4 and Class 3
  • Materials of construction and corrosion review
  • Safety, HAZOP-style and management-of-change reviews
  • Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) studies of key equipment

Related: Process engineering and scale-up · Engineering CAPEX and OPEX

Biodiesel producerIn progress

Plant review: economics, HAZOP and P&ID assessment

An independent review of an operating biodiesel plant, covering its economics, its process safety and the quality of its engineering drawings.

Scope

  • Economic analysis of plant operations: feedstock, yields, utilities and operating costs
  • Improvement options compared by cost, benefit and payback
  • Hazard and operability study (HAZOP) of the process, section by section
  • Review of existing safeguards and recommended actions for each hazard identified
  • Assessment of piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs) against the plant as built and good practice
  • List of drawing discrepancies and the corrections needed
  • Prioritized recommendations for safety, reliability and cost
