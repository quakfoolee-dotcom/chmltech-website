Overview

What does it take to compete in manufacturing today?

Your competitive edge depends on improving operating margins, reducing overhead costs and decreasing capital requirements while customers demand more. Chmltech looks beyond processes to products and people, including staff performance, management behaviour and use of technology. Through interviews with employees and managers, document reviews and direct observation on the shop floor, we target efficiency, cost and quality through effective organizational design. We target significant, measurable productivity gains and track them against your baseline KPIs. Most importantly, your team leads the change, building a culture of continuous improvement.

Signs it's time to act

  • OEE is unknown or well below what the equipment should deliver
  • Changeovers take longer than they should and vary by crew
  • Work-in-process piles up between operations
  • Overtime and expediting are part of every week
  • Scrap and rework are absorbed rather than measured

Industry challenges

What we see in manufacturing.

Margin pressure

Global competition and rising input costs squeeze operating margins, while overhead and capital needs keep growing.

Hidden capacity losses

Unplanned stops, slow changeovers and minor stoppages quietly erode OEE and force extra shifts or capital spending.

Quality and delivery expectations

Customers expect higher quality and faster, more reliable delivery, leaving little room for scrap, rework or late orders.

Improvement that does not stick

Past initiatives faded because they were driven by outsiders or a report, not by the people who run the work.

How we help

How we help

KPIs and OEE measurement

We define key metrics for service levels, productivity, speed and quality, including OEE, and set the baseline used to measure every improvement.

Value stream and current-state review

We map key processes, analyze financial and operating data, observe the floor directly and review your management tools to design sustainable solutions.

Waste and bottleneck analysis

Process observations separate value-added from non-value-added activity, exposing the constraints that limit your manufacturing capacity.

Changeover and flow improvement

We apply Lean tools such as SMED, standard work and cell layout to shorten changeovers, cut WIP and improve flow.

5S and workplace organization

A complete 5S assessment based on Lean and Six Sigma methods to discard waste and create a clean, safe and efficient workplace.

Lean workshops and change management

Introductory Lean thinking workshops, a change readiness assessment and management tools that turn strategy into daily activities everyone understands.

Typical scenarios

What improvement looks like.

Illustrative examples of the kind of change we work on.

Changeover on a packaging line

Before: Each format change took most of an hour, with crews searching for parts and adjusting by trial and error.

After: SMED analysis, pre-staged kits and a standard sequence make changeovers shorter and more consistent.

WIP in a 60-person fabrication shop

Before: Parts sat in piles between cutting, welding and paint, hiding quality problems and delaying orders.

After: A simple pull system and WIP limits shorten lead time and surface defects sooner.

Daily production meeting

Before: Supervisors argued over whose numbers were right and problems were discussed but not owned.

After: A 15-minute huddle at a visual KPI board assigns owners and due dates for every issue.

Questions

Frequently asked

What productivity improvement can we expect?

Every operation is different, so we do not promise a fixed number. We set a baseline for your KPIs at the start, agree targets with you and track measurable gains against that baseline throughout.

How do you work with our shop floor team?

We interview employees and managers, observe processes directly and run hands-on workshops. Your team helps design and test the changes, which is what makes them stick.

Do we need new equipment or software?

Usually not to start. Most early gains come from better flow, faster changeovers and standard work. Where technology or automation helps, we recommend it based on your data.

Can you run a 5S assessment on its own?

Yes. A 5S assessment is a practical starting point and often reveals quick wins in safety, organization and time lost searching for tools and materials.
