Privacy notice

How Chmltech Ltd. handles the information you share on this website.

Last updated November 12, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Chmltech Ltd., British Columbia, Canada. Questions about this notice or your information can be sent to info [at] chmltech [dot] com.

What we collect and why

  • Contact and booking forms: your name, email address and the details you enter (for example company, phone, topic and message), so we can reply to your enquiry or booking request.
  • Journal PDF and template downloads: your name, email address, company (optional), the paper or file you requested and whether you asked for updates. We use this to record the request, understand which research and tools are useful to readers and, where relevant, follow up about your request.
  • Insights subscription: your email address, to send the updates you asked for.

We only send ongoing updates about new research and services to people who have asked for them, for example by ticking the updates box on a download form or subscribing. Every update email will tell you how to stop receiving them.

Service providers

  • Web3Forms receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Microsoft Clarity measures how the website is used: pages visited, clicks, scrolling, device and browser type, and approximate location. We use it to see which pages and articles are useful and where the site is hard to use. Clarity hides what you type into form fields, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors. Microsoft processes this data under its own privacy statement (privacy.microsoft.com).

We do not sell your personal information, and this website does not show advertising or use advertising tracking.

Information stored in your browser

To make the site easier to use, your browser stores a small amount of information locally: progress in the free courses, and the name and email you entered on a download form so you don't have to type them again. This stays on your device; you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Microsoft Clarity may also set cookies to recognise a returning visit; you can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the site works the same without them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and download requests for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our business records, and we delete them on request where we are not required to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to updates, by writing to info [at] chmltech [dot] com. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).
