Services

Key activities during the project

Key performance metrics (KPIs)

A framework of key metrics for service level, productivity, speed and quality for key processes, used to set the baseline and measure success.

Mapping of key processes

Process maps using industry-standard BPMN 2.0, built by interviewing managers and key employees, including information flows, roles and system touch points.

Review of current practices and tools

A thorough as-is analysis: process mapping, financial and operating data, direct observation and review of management tools.

Value-added analysis

Process observations that quantify customer value-added and non-value-added activities and reveal hidden capacity.

Team lead roles and responsibilities

Time with team leads to understand their responsibilities and increase active management time.

Management tools

Tools that measure and display KPIs so managers can run day-to-day operations with facts.

Implemented method changes

Quick wins and longer-term changes that make processes scalable, cost-effective and quality oriented, implemented with you.

Lean

Powerful Lean methodology

Our Lean process improvement delivers real and sustained results, typically in 15–25 weeks. We embed ourselves in your organization and customize our improvement toolkit to go beyond one-time fixes. We know the hardest part is implementation, so we stay with you through it, coaching your managers to use the management tools and KPIs to improve day-to-day operations in their areas.

People

Your team are the key drivers of change

Your people are mentored every step of the way: through interviews, process mapping, observing processes and developing ideas for improvement. This engaging approach moves the organization through the change management cycle and makes sure the improvements stick. We provide the kick-start to your Lean journey.

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does a process improvement program take?

Our Lean programs typically deliver results in 15–25 weeks, depending on scope. Quick wins usually come much earlier, during the first phases.

Will our operations be disrupted?

We work alongside your team and plan changes around operating needs. Most work happens through observation, workshops and small, tested changes.

What will our people learn?

Process mapping, waste identification, problem solving, standard work and running operations with KPIs. These skills remain after the project.

How do we know it worked?

We set a KPI baseline at the start and track service level, productivity, speed and quality through the program, so results are measurable.
