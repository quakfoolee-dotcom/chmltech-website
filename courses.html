Course FAQs

Can a course be run privately for our team?

Yes. Most courses are delivered in-house so we can use your own processes and examples. Contact us with your group size and preferred format.

Do courses include a real project?

Where possible, yes. Applying the methods to a real problem during the course is the fastest way to build skill and show results.

Can courses be delivered online?

Yes. Every course can be delivered as live virtual sessions or as a blend of virtual and on-site work.

How do we register?

Use the Enquire button on any course or contact us. We will confirm dates, format and fees.
