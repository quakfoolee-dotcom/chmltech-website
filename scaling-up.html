Overview

Why growth gets harder, not easier

In many growing companies, each new customer, employee or location adds complexity faster than it adds profit. Processes that worked with ten people fail with fifty, the owner becomes the bottleneck, and cash gets tight even as sales rise. Scaling well means building capacity ahead of growth: in processes, systems, people and finances.

Signs you are hitting a ceiling

  • Sales are growing but profit is not
  • The owner or a few key people approve everything
  • Orders, quotes or projects slip through the cracks
  • Spreadsheets and email hold the business together
  • Cash is tight despite a full order book
