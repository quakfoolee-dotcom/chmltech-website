Many growth-company leaders reach a point where they actually dread adding another customer, employee, or location. It feels like they are just adding more weight to an ever-heavier anchor they are dragging up the hill. The Scaling Up performance framework helps businesses of scaleups how to achieve it, no matter how bug and complicated a business becomes.
