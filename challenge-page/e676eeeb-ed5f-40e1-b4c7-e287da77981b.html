About this course

This program provides a structured approach to evaluating and preparing your organization for artificial intelligence within an operational excellence framework. It focuses on the technical, cultural, and strategic requirements needed to move beyond small pilot projects to scalable implementation. Participants will learn how to audit data quality, assess workforce capabilities, and define clear performance metrics for AI initiatives. The course is designed for leaders aiming to drive digital transformation through evidence-based planning and sound governance in industries ranging from manufacturing to professional services.

What it includes

  • 24 guided steps you complete at your own pace
  • Practical frameworks and tools you can apply at work
  • Free of charge
