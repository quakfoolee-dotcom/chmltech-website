Rapid Lean assessment: a two-week operations review

Find out where your improvement opportunities are, in two weeks.

A short, focused review of your operation across people, processes and performance measures. You get a clear picture of where waste and delay sit, which quick wins to take first, and what a larger improvement program could achieve.

Overview

How the assessment works

We spend time with your team where the work happens: interviews, process walk-throughs and a day in the life of key roles. In parallel we analyze your cost and revenue drivers and current capacity. We then estimate the size of the opportunity from your own data and agree priorities with you. The estimate is specific to your operation; we do not quote a typical figure in advance.

Signs you need this

  • You know there is waste but not where to start
  • You want an outside view before committing to a larger project
  • Leadership disagrees about what the real problems are
  • You need a business case for improvement investment

Services

What the assessment covers

People

Roles, workload, skills and how decisions are made.

Processes

Walk-throughs and maps of the main value streams, with delays and waste marked.

Performance measures

What is measured today, what should be, and the current baseline.

Financial analysis

Cost and revenue drivers, and an estimate of the improvement opportunity from your data.

Quick wins

Changes you can make in the next few weeks with little cost.

Roadmap

A prioritized plan of larger improvements, with effort and expected benefit.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we have to commit to a larger project?

No. The assessment stands on its own. Many clients carry out the quick wins themselves.

What do you need from us?

Access to the operation, a few hours of each key person's time, and recent operational and financial data.

Can it be done remotely?

Partly. Interviews and data analysis can be remote, but walking the process in person gives much better results.
