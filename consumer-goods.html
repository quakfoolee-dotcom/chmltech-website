Overview

Can you grow margins and the customer experience at the same time?

In consumer goods, rising input costs, retailer demands and shifting consumer tastes squeeze margins from every side. Cutting costs alone can hurt the experience that keeps customers buying. We engage your operations directly, observing work as it happens on the floor, in the warehouse and across the supply chain to find non-value-added time and inefficient processes. Then we build customer-centric operating measures that give your team daily data on the value delivered. With a customized continuous improvement framework, your people can control processing costs, improve production efficiency and respond faster to the market.

Signs it's time to act

  • Product margins are shrinking even as sales volumes hold
  • Changeovers between SKUs eat into production time
  • Retailer fill rate or on-time delivery targets are often missed
  • Supervisors spend more time firefighting than managing
  • You track output, but not the value customers receive

Industry challenges

What we see in consumer goods.

Margin pressure

Rising ingredient, packaging and freight costs, plus retailer pricing pressure, leave little room for waste in production and fulfilment.

SKU complexity

More variants, pack sizes and promotions mean more changeovers, more inventory and more chances for errors.

Disconnected supply chain

When purchasing, production and shipping plan separately, the result is stockouts, excess inventory and expedited freight.

Little active management time

Team leads and managers are pulled into admin and urgent issues, leaving little time to coach people and improve processes.

Typical scenarios

What improvement looks like.

Illustrative examples of the kind of change we work on.

Changeover on a packaging line

Before: changeovers between SKUs took most of an hour and were done differently by each crew.

After: a standard changeover checklist with prep done while the line runs cut downtime and made results consistent.

Order fulfilment at a specialty food producer

Before: retailer orders were often short-shipped because production plans ignored actual orders.

After: a weekly sales and operations check aligned production with orders and improved fill rate.

Supervisor workload at a personal care manufacturer

Before: team leads spent much of each shift on paperwork and chasing parts.

After: simplified reporting and a parts kitting routine gave them more time to coach and solve problems on the floor.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work with food and beverage producers?

Yes. Our methods apply to food, beverage, personal care and household goods producers, and we respect your food safety and quality requirements when changing processes.

How quickly will we see results?

We look for quick wins that need no new systems early in the project, such as standard changeovers or simpler reporting. Larger changes follow a plan with milestones measured against your baseline.

Can you help with SKU rationalization?

Yes. We analyze margin, volume and complexity by SKU so you can see which products earn their place and which drive cost through changeovers and inventory.

What does a team lead role assessment involve?

We observe how team leads and managers spend their time, then identify admin and firefighting that can be reduced so they spend more time actively managing people and processes.
