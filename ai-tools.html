    Method and FAQ

    How this map is built.

    How current is this list?

    Every tool was checked against its vendor's own website and pricing page in November 2026. Prices are list prices in US dollars unless marked, and vendors change them often, so confirm on the vendor's page before you buy.

    What does cost per output mean?

    For each category we picked the unit the work is paid in: a seat per month for most business software, an image, a minute of video, a million tokens, an hour of transcription, a resolved support conversation. The cost is the entry paid plan's price divided by what that plan includes, or the vendor's metered rate. Where credits are opaque, the price is contact-sales, or the plan's units are unclear, we say "Not comparable" instead of guessing. Prices that were promotions at check time are marked as such.

    How was the list checked?

    The list was rebuilt category by category from vendors' own pages, with AI-assisted research. Two independent reviews then re-checked every website, every acquisition and shutdown claim, prices for the best-known and flagged tools, a random sample of entries, newly added tools and the Canada claims. Errors found were corrected before publishing.

    What do the Canada filters mean?

    "Data can stay in Canada" means the vendor documents a Canadian hosting region or data-residency option for the product, sometimes only on certain plans (see the note on each tool). "Data in Canada if self-hosted" means you can run it on your own Canadian servers or cloud account. These were researched for the most-used tools; Unknown means not confirmed, not no.

    Is Chmltech paid to list these tools?

    No. No vendor paid to be listed, and listing is not an endorsement. Product names and logos belong to their owners and are shown only to identify the products.
