Overview

How do you stay competitive with finite resources?

Mining, forestry, aggregates and other resource operations face volatile markets, remote sites, tight labour pools and rising expectations on safety and environmental stewardship. Chmltech finds the places in your organization where you can accelerate performance, reduce costs and improve both delivery and margins. Our immersive approach brings together operations, maintenance and management to build practical workforce plans, sound maintenance scheduling and better communication across sites. We start by stabilizing the basics, such as clearing the backlog, and then put scalable processes in place that support future growth.

Signs it's time to act

  • Maintenance backlog keeps rising and equipment availability suffers
  • Crews wait on parts, equipment or instructions at the start of shifts
  • You cannot react quickly when commodity prices or demand shift
  • Safety, maintenance and production plans live in separate silos
  • Remote sites and head office work from different information

Industry challenges

What we see in natural resources.

Volatile markets

Commodity price and demand swings mean operations must scale up or down quickly without losing control of cost or safety.

Maintenance backlog

Deferred maintenance on mobile and fixed equipment drives breakdowns, lowers availability and pushes crews into reactive work.

Workforce planning

Skilled trades are hard to find and retain, and rosters, skills and workloads are rarely planned together.

Communication across sites

Information moves slowly between remote sites, contractors and head office, so decisions are made on stale data.

How we help

How we help

Holistic workforce planning

Typical scenarios

What improvement looks like.

Illustrative examples of the kind of change we work on.

Haul truck maintenance at an open-pit site

Before: Trucks waited in the shop while mechanics searched for parts and tooling.

After: Kitted parts and a weekly schedule let jobs start on time and trucks return to service sooner.

Sawmill shift start-up

Before: Crews lost the first part of each shift waiting for instructions and equipment checks.

After: A short start-of-shift huddle and standard checklists get lines running promptly and consistently.

Remote camp and site logistics

Before: Supply orders were placed by phone and email, which caused emergency flights for missing items.

After: A simple shared order and reorder process gives site and head office the same view of stock.

Questions

Frequently asked

What types of resource companies do you work with?

We work with growing Canadian companies in sectors such as mining, forestry, aggregates and related services. The common thread is a need for safe, reliable operations and strong maintenance and workflow management.

How do you return value on the investment?

Value comes from increased customer value and the elimination of waste, such as idle time, rework and emergency repairs. We agree an ROI target and how it will be measured before the project starts.

Can you work with remote or multi-site operations?

Yes. We combine on-site observation and workshops with remote follow-up, and we focus on processes and communication that connect sites, contractors and head office.

Do you offer training for our crews?

Yes. We often deliver an introductory Lean thinking workshop covering improvement concepts and the change management process, and we can add 5S training as part of a workplace assessment.
