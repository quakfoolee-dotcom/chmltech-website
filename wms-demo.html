What you can try

A full warehouse system, not a slideshow.

Every screen works with the same sample records, so a change in one module shows up everywhere else.

Inventory and units

Stock by location and lot, FIFO cost layers, reorder rules, transfers, cycle counts and traceability. Sample products come in Each, Pack of 3, Carton of 12 and Pallet of 480.

Purchasing and receiving

Purchase orders, receiving with quality inspection holds, directed putaway, supplier returns and purchase cost reconciliation.

Sales and fulfilment

Quotes, sales orders, kits, picking, packing and shipping, with customer returns and packing slips.

Manufacturing

Multi-level bills of materials, manufacture orders, material issue, labour approval and a production schedule.

Accounting views

Invoices, supplier bills, credits, payments, inventory journals, stock reconciliation and historical valuation.

Dashboards and reports

Business and warehouse dashboards, demand forecasting and a report centre built from the same records.

Phone scanning screens

Mobile pages for lookup, receive, move, pick, pack, ship and count, plus a document scanner that opens records by barcode.

Two layouts, four languages

A classic record-and-tab layout or a modern layout, in English, French, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese.

Try it in 10 minutes

Follow one order from the dock to the customer.

Each link opens the demo at that screen. The demo's help button (?) shows the same walkthrough.

  1. 01

    Receive stock

    Open Purchasing and receive the sample purchase order: two cartons of the insulated travel bottle, which is 24 bottles.

    Open Purchasing →
  2. 02

    Check units of measure

    In Inventory, see how Each, Pack of 3, Carton of 12 and Pallet of 480 convert, and edit a unit if you like.

    Open Inventory →
  3. 03

    Issue a sales order

    Open the draft sales order for two packs (6 bottles) and issue it. The order now needs stock reserved and picked.

    Open Sales orders →
  4. 04

    Pick the order

    In Picking, reserve (commit) the stock and work the pick through to Finished. A shipment is created.

    Open Picking →
  5. 05

    Pack and ship

    In Shipping, confirm packing and ship. On-hand stock falls by 6 bottles and the order is fulfilled.

    Open Shipping →
  6. 06

    Start again

    Use Reset demo at the top of the demo whenever you want the original sample warehouse back.

Your data

The demo runs entirely in your browser.

The demo app has no server. Your sample warehouse lives in your own browser, so you can experiment freely. Privacy.

Launch the WMS demo
  • Your warehouse records are saved in this browser on this device, not on a server.
  • Other browsers, devices and visitors never see your changes, and devices do not sync.
  • Reset demo restores the sample warehouse at any time. Clearing your browser data also removes it.
  • You can download the demo data as a JSON file, or the product catalog as a CSV file.
  • There are no accounts, passwords or automatic backups. Every visitor uses the Demo visitor role with full access.
  • QuickBooks, EDI, carrier, email and payment connections are switched off. Please do not enter real customer data.
  • Like the rest of the site, the demo uses Microsoft Clarity analytics to see which screens visitors use, with record details masked.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the WMS demo free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, download or payment.

Where is my demo data stored?

Only in your browser's storage on the device you are using; your records are not saved on any server. Clearing your browser data or pressing Reset demo removes them. Like the rest of chmltech.com, the demo uses Microsoft Clarity analytics, with record details masked; see our Privacy page.

Can I try it on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The main screens work on a phone, and the demo includes phone pages for scanning, receiving, picking, packing and counting. Each device keeps its own separate sample warehouse.

Can I use Chmltech WMS for my own warehouse?

The demo runs on fictional records saved in your browser, so it is not set up for live operations. If you would like to talk about using Chmltech WMS in your business, or you are choosing between WMS options, book a call and we will help you work out what fits.

Which languages does it support?

English, French, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Choose the language at the top of the demo.
