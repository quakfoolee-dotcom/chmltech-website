Case Study: Two of the Keys to Walmart's Success

Quak Foo LeeSep 6, 20211 min read

As the retired Chief Operating Officer of Walmart, Don Soderquist is often asked, "How has Walmart done it?" or "What's the secret?" His response is that there is no secret, no magic formula. In fact, the principles that launched Walmart into unprecedented success are so simple it's easy to overlook their significance. Don Soderquist was part of the leadership team that grew the company from $1 billion in sales to $244 billion (the largest company in the world at that time). This video takes an inside look at two of the principles that guided one of the greatest businesses of all time.
