Boost Your Business with CDAP: Funding Support for Digital Adoption

Quak Foo LeeApr 16, 20231 min read

Are you a small or medium-sized enterprise (SME) looking to improve your productivity and competitiveness through digital adoption? Look no further than the Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP). As an approved CDAP digital advisor, Chmltech Ltd. is here to help you access this valuable funding support.

The CDAP program provides funding of up to 90% of the eligible cost of retaining the services of a digital advisor, up to a maximum grant value of $15,000 per SME, to develop a digital adoption plan. In addition, SMEs can access a 0% interest loan of up to $100,000 through the Business Development Bank of Canada (BDC) and up to $7,300 in funded work placement to hire talent through a work placement.

Chmltech Ltd. is a digital technology consultant company with a team of experienced and knowledgeable advisors who can guide you through the process of digital adoption. We can help you identify and select the right digital technologies for your business, provide training and support to your employees, and help you implement best practices for digital adoption.

Don't miss out on this opportunity to boost your business with CDAP funding support. Contact Chmltech Ltd. today to learn more about how we can help you access this valuable program.
