10 Best ERP Systems for Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises

Quak Foo LeeMar 12, 20233 min read

As a small or medium-sized enterprise (SME), managing your business processes efficiently can be a challenge, especially when you have a limited number of employees. Fortunately, implementing an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system can help streamline your operations and increase productivity. However, choosing the right ERP system can be overwhelming. In this blog post, we’ll provide you with ten of the best ERP systems for SMEs, along with their features, cost, pros and cons, and integration and growth factors.

  1. SAP Business One SAP Business One is a comprehensive ERP system that offers a range of features, including finance, sales, customer relationship management, inventory management, and manufacturing. The cost starts at $10,000 for the first user, with additional users costing $3,000 each. Pros include a robust set of features, strong customer support, and scalability. Cons include a steep learning curve and a high price tag.
  2. Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central is an all-in-one cloud-based ERP system that provides financial management, supply chain management, sales, project management, and more. The cost starts at $70 per user per month. Pros include seamless integration with other Microsoft products, user-friendly interface, and customizable dashboards. Cons include limited customization options and lack of advanced inventory management features.
  3. Odoo Odoo is an open-source ERP system that offers features like accounting, project management, inventory management, and e-commerce. The basic version is free, while the enterprise version starts at $20 per user per month. Pros include the ability to customize modules to fit specific business needs, easy to use interface, and a large community of developers. Cons include limited support and documentation for the free version.
  4. NetSuite NetSuite is a cloud-based ERP system that provides features such as financial management, inventory management, e-commerce, and customer relationship management. The cost starts at $999 per month for the basic package. Pros include a robust set of features, scalability, and easy customization. Cons include a high price tag and a complex setup process.
  5. Sage Intacct Sage Intacct is a cloud-based ERP system that offers features such as financial management, inventory management, and project management. The cost starts at $400 per month for the basic package. Pros include a user-friendly interface, easy customization, and seamless integration with other Sage products. Cons include limited features for manufacturing and e-commerce.
  6. Epicor ERP Epicor ERP is a comprehensive ERP system that offers features such as financial management, supply chain management, production management, and customer relationship management. The cost varies depending on the package selected. Pros include a customizable dashboard, powerful reporting capabilities, and scalability. Cons include a steep learning curve and limited customization options.
  7. Acumatica Acumatica is a cloud-based ERP system that offers features such as financial management, inventory management, and project management. The cost starts at $5,000 per year. Pros include a user-friendly interface, easy customization, and scalable pricing. Cons include limited features for manufacturing and a limited number of integrations.
  8. Infor CloudSuite Infor CloudSuite is a cloud-based ERP system that provides features such as financial management, inventory management, and customer relationship management. The cost varies depending on the package selected. Pros include a comprehensive set of features, scalability, and easy customization. Cons include a complex interface and limited customization options.
  9. IQMS ERP IQMS ERP is a comprehensive ERP system that offers features such as financial management, inventory management, and production management. The cost varies depending on the package selected. Pros include a robust set of features for manufacturing, strong customer support, and real-time data monitoring. Cons include a high price tag and limited scalability.
  10. SYSPRO ERP SYSPRO ERP is a comprehensive ERP system that provides features such as financial management, inventory management, and production management. The cost varies depending on the package selected. Pros include a customizable dashboard, strong customer support, and scalability. Cons include a complex interface and limited integrations.

In conclusion, implementing an ERP system can greatly benefit small and medium-sized enterprises with 5-20 employees. However, it’s important to choose the right system that fits your specific business needs. We’ve provided you with ten of the best ERP systems for SMEs with their features, cost, pros and cons, and integration and growth factors to help you make an informed decision. Take your time, research each option thoroughly, and choose the one that’s right for your business.
