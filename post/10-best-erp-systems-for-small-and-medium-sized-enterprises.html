10 ERP Systems for Small and Mid-Sized Businesses in 2026: Fit and Pricing Models

Quak Foo LeeNov 13, 20264 min read

This list was first published in 2023 with prices that are now out of date. It has been rewritten for 2026 with the pricing model of each system, the published price where one exists, and the kind of business each fits best. It is not a ranking: the right system depends on your processes, your industry and, above all, the implementation partner. For how to choose, see our guide to choosing and implementing an ERP.

Prices are in the vendor's own currency and exclude implementation, which usually costs more than the first year's licences. Several vendors change list prices every year and offer first-year discounts, so treat the published figures as a starting point and get written quotes for the same scope.

What has changed since 2023

  • Prices rose. Microsoft raised Business Central list prices on 1 November 2025, its first significant increase in more than five years: Essentials from US$70 to US$80 and Premium from US$100 to US$110 per user per month. Odoo also raises its regional prices periodically.
  • Names changed. Epicor ERP is now sold as Epicor Kinetic, and IQMS, bought by Dassault Systèmes in 2019, is now DELMIAworks.
  • Pricing models diverged. Per-user pricing (Business Central, Odoo, NetSuite) favours firms with a few heavy users. Acumatica's resource-based model, with unlimited users, favours firms where many people need occasional access, such as warehouse or field staff.
  • AI features are now standard in sales pitches. Ask to see them working on your own data during the scripted demo, and ask whether they cost extra.

How to narrow the list

  1. Start with your industry. Manufacturers need production planning, bills of materials and shop-floor data; distributors need warehouse and pricing depth; project and service firms need project accounting.
  2. Check the local partner. A capable partner in your province with experience in your industry matters more than small feature differences.
  3. Model five years of cost, including your own staff time, for two or three finalists.
  4. Run the same scripted demo for each finalist using your own data.
