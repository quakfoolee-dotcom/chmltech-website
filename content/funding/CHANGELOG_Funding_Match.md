## Draft V4 — 2026-11-20 (not published): optional exact project cost
- **Exact project cost (optional)** box under the project-cost ranges. A valid figure picks the matching range automatically and replaces the assumed midpoint (for example $4M for "Over $2 million") in the funding stack, the minimum-project-size checks, the results lead ("for a $275,000 project") and the PDF ("$275,000 (your figure)").
- Input accepts commas and a $ sign; text or amounts under $1,000 show a message and hold Next until fixed or cleared. Picking a range that does not contain the figure clears it with a message, so the two never disagree. The figure is kept when going Back.
- Without a figure, the stack now says the amount is "assumed at" the range midpoint and invites an exact cost.
- **Report service (Cloudflare worker `chmltech-report`) Version 27** delivered for deployment: same worker, address and secrets; adds Funding Match email wording (`report_kind=funding`); Digital Assessment emails unchanged except the signature now reads Vancouver, BC.
- Tests: exact-cost flow (invalid input, range sync, range change, Back), 18 personas, 6,240-profile run with no errors, mobile 390px with no overflow.

## Draft V2 — 2026-11-20 (not published): red-team QC fixes
Four independent red-team reviews (eligibility rules A and B, match results, code/UX/report): 128 findings (32 Critical, 50 Major, 46 Minor). All Critical and Major findings fixed, plus most Minor ones; reports in the session test folder.
- **Eligibility data:** 118 rule corrections checked against official pages (`content/funding/redteam_corrections_v2.json`, applied by `enrich_v1.py` after auto-extraction). Wrong stream limits removed (PacifiCan/FedDev RAII, Growing Greenhouses, Yukon Venture Loan, Scale AI, Manitoba IGP/SBVCTC), non-profits no longer wrongly excluded (Canada Music Fund, ESSOR, Yukon rebates, Quebec Fisheries Fund, RTRI and others), missing rules added (BDC Small Business Loan 24 months and $100K revenue, CDAEIA 6 employees, Canada Summer Jobs 2 years, Ontario Job Grant 1 year, project minimums, corporation-only tax credits).
- **New rule types:** identity programs need every listed identity (`req_who`: Indigenous women, women, Black, youth, newcomer, Indigenous); Ontario sub-regions (`region`: Northern, Eastern, Southwestern, Southern); maximum business age (`yrs_max`); excluded legal forms (`legal_excl`: co-ops out of NRC IRAP, sole proprietors out of OTTF); limits that apply to businesses only (`scope`); special-situation programs (`niche`: tariffs, defence, large energy users, CCUS, Work-Sharing, greenhouse, large buildings); per-participant amounts (`per_unit`); investor-only credits.
- **Matching:** size tags must match (Exporter or Rural can no longer stand in for SME size); paused programs move to "Watch for the next intake"; loan-led programs grouped as loans; R&D and clean-tech focus, sector and farm-only programs penalised for the wrong goal or industry; score rebalanced to a 100-point scale (no more 99s for weak fits); single-purpose programs (IRAP, SR&ED, Ontario Job Grant) no longer buried.
- **New questions:** "Which part of Ontario?" (Ontario only) and "Do any of these situations apply?" (U.S. tariffs, defence supplier, large energy user). New industry option "Food and beverage processing" (matches agri-food and manufacturing). "Just me" counts as one employee.
- **Funding stack:** no invented amounts ("Amount varies" or "Up to $X per participant"); no $0 rows; repayable money shown separately from non-repayable; program stacking caps respected; assumed project cost stated; cash warning only when grants are counted.
- **Copy:** "fit score 78/100" (not a %, "not a chance of approval"); near misses no longer promise eligibility; corrected incorporation note; Canadian -ize spelling; grammar fixes.
- **Accessibility and UX:** focus moves to each new question (keyboard users were stuck), live question counter, aria-current step, hints linked to questions, pick limit announced, "Show more" shows all matches, "See in directory" clears filters first, Start again resets the form, no mobile overflow, chip contrast fixed, page anchors and hero spacing restored.
- **Report:** PDF links now "Official program page" (no broken wrapped URLs), program entries kept together, hanging indents, œ kept, fuller "Your answers", disclaimer on page 1. Fallback email now carries the full results.
- **Worker V27:** header updated, Digital Assessment owner subject unchanged ("Digital Assessment report"); signature reads Vancouver, BC for both reports.
- Test hook only with `?fmtest=1`; cache-busters on the new JS and CSS.

## Draft V1 — 2026-11-20 (not published)
- **Guided funding match on the Funding page** (`funding.html`): new hero ("Find the grants, tax credits and loans your business can apply for.") with **Start the free funding match** and "or browse all 408 programs →"; the program directory stays below unchanged.
- **Five steps, 13 questions** (`assets/js/funding-match.js`): province, industry, legal form, employees, revenue, years operating; what to fund (up to 3), project cost, start date; groups such as Indigenous-, women- or Black-owned (optional); funding types and cash share; readiness.
- **Matching:** province (federal programs included where available), industry, who can apply and purpose must fit; stated rules (incorporation, employees, revenue, years operating, minimum project size, for-profit/non-profit) rule a program out or make it a near miss; unknown rules never rule a program out. Fit score: purpose 40, industry 20, funding type 15, amount 15, intake status 10, plus small adjustments (targeted groups, R&D or clean-tech focus, sector programs, already-started projects, low-confidence data).
- **Results:** counts by type; a funding stack (one program per chosen type, indicative amounts, 75% stacking cap unless a program states lower, warning when the visitor's cash share is short); best matches by type with fit %, why it fits, check before you apply, official link and "See in directory"; near misses; closed programs to watch; readiness checklist.
- **Report:** PDF (jsPDF) and the same email form as the Digital Assessment (Turnstile, consent, report service). `main.js` sends `report_kind`; the report worker (`content/worker/chmltech-report-worker.js`) now words the email for either report and signs from Vancouver, BC. **The worker must be redeployed** before funding reports are emailed with the right wording.
- **Data:** `content/funding/enrich_v1.py` builds `rules_v1.json` from the eligibility text with conservative patterns plus manual overrides (CanExport SMEs, NRC IRAP, SR&ED). Coverage is thin (about 15 to 50 programs per rule), so near misses appear for few profiles. Phase 1 (agents reading every official page, red-team spot checks) replaces it before publication.
- **Draft builder:** `content/funding/draft_v1.py` patches the built `funding.html`; on publication the markup moves into `content/gen/grants_page.py`.
- Tested with 8 personas (BC sole proprietor retail, Alberta 40-person manufacturer, Quebec software start-up, Ontario Indigenous-owned tourism, BC engineering exporter, Nova Scotia non-profit, Manitoba pre-start founder, Ontario 300-person automotive): no script errors, PDF builds.
