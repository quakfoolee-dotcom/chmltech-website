# Funding Match — changelog

## Draft V1 — 2026-11-20 (not published)
- **Guided funding match on the Funding page** (`funding.html`): new hero ("Find the grants, tax credits and loans your business can apply for.") with **Start the free funding match** and "or browse all 408 programs →"; the program directory stays below unchanged.
- **Five steps, 13 questions** (`assets/js/funding-match.js`): province, industry, legal form, employees, revenue, years operating; what to fund (up to 3), project cost, start date; groups such as Indigenous-, women- or Black-owned (optional); funding types and cash share; readiness.
- **Matching:** province (federal programs included where available), industry, who can apply and purpose must fit; stated rules (incorporation, employees, revenue, years operating, minimum project size, for-profit/non-profit) rule a program out or make it a near miss; unknown rules never rule a program out. Fit score: purpose 40, industry 20, funding type 15, amount 15, intake status 10, plus small adjustments (targeted groups, R&D or clean-tech focus, sector programs, already-started projects, low-confidence data).
- **Results:** counts by type; a funding stack (one program per chosen type, indicative amounts, 75% stacking cap unless a program states lower, warning when the visitor's cash share is short); best matches by type with fit %, why it fits, check before you apply, official link and "See in directory"; near misses; closed programs to watch; readiness checklist.
- **Report:** PDF (jsPDF) and the same email form as the Digital Assessment (Turnstile, consent, report service). `main.js` sends `report_kind`; the report worker (`content/worker/chmltech-report-worker.js`) now words the email for either report and signs from Vancouver, BC. **The worker must be redeployed** before funding reports are emailed with the right wording.
- **Data:** `content/funding/enrich_v1.py` builds `rules_v1.json` from the eligibility text with conservative patterns plus manual overrides (CanExport SMEs, NRC IRAP, SR&ED). Coverage is thin (about 15 to 50 programs per rule), so near misses appear for few profiles. Phase 1 (agents reading every official page, red-team spot checks) replaces it before publication.
- **Draft builder:** `content/funding/draft_v1.py` patches the built `funding.html`; on publication the markup moves into `content/gen/grants_page.py`.
- Tested with 8 personas (BC sole proprietor retail, Alberta 40-person manufacturer, Quebec software start-up, Ontario Indigenous-owned tourism, BC engineering exporter, Nova Scotia non-profit, Manitoba pre-start founder, Ontario 300-person automotive): no script errors, PDF builds.
