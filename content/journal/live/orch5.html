1 · Topology

Centralized orchestrator

Control
One accountable lead
Communication
Star: lead to workers and back
State
Lead-owned ledger and worker artifacts
Allocation
Fixed or rule-based routing by the lead
Chief hazard
Lead bottleneck; misdelegation
2 · Topology

Hierarchical orchestration

Control
Supervisors at several levels
Communication
Tree: down and up through each level
State
Tiered state and summaries
Allocation
Each supervisor routes within its domain
Chief hazard
Summary loss; management overhead
3 · Topology

Decentralized mesh

Control
Distributed peers and a shared protocol
Communication
Direct peer-to-peer messages
State
Federated or shared state
Allocation
Peers hand work to one another
Chief hazard
Cycles, conflicting owners, uncontrolled chatter
4 · Mechanism

Blackboard

Control
A separate scheduler or event-driven
Communication
Publish–subscribe through the board
State
Shared, versioned workspace
Allocation
Agents act when the board holds work they can do
Chief hazard
Stale or corrupted shared state
5 · Mechanism

Contract net (market-based)

Control
Task announcer plus eligible bidders
Communication
Announce, bid, award, execute
State
Award record held by the manager
Allocation
Capability bidding and award
Chief hazard
Auction overhead; poor bid signals
