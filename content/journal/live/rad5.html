The five stages side by side. Choose a stage above to watch it step by step.

1 · Collect

Research

Responsibility
Find, classify, date and verify sources
Output
Evidence manifest and fact register
Gate
Required categories covered; unknowns identified
Failure to watch
Source drift: an old product page treated as current
2 · Model

Analyze

Responsibility
Derive findings, test alternatives, quantify trade-offs
Output
Analysis workbook and assumption register
Gate
Methods and critical calculations reproducible
Failure to watch
Analysis drift: a reported estimate becomes an observed value
3 · Compose

Draft

Responsibility
Turn supported findings into a readable deliverable
Output
Versioned draft with claim references
Gate
Material claims linked to evidence or labelled inference
Failure to watch
Citation laundering: a source cited for a claim it does not support
4 · Verify

Review

Responsibility
Challenge accuracy, completeness, risks and limits
Output
Issue register: pass, rework, escalate or stop
Gate
No unresolved blocking issue
Failure to watch
Review correlation: the reviewer repeats the same model's error
5 · Release

Finalize

Responsibility
Apply authorized edits, lock version, prepare release
Output
Final artifact and change log
Gate
Exact artifact approved for a stated destination
Failure to watch
Release drift: the artifact changes after approval
