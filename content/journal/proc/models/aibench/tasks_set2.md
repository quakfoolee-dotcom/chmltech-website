# Process engineering calculation tasks, set 2

Answer every task. For each, give the numeric answers under the keys listed. Where a task gives no data for a property, use your own engineering knowledge. Use 1/0 for yes/no keys.

## Task 21
No property data are given. What is the vapour pressure of toluene at 80 C, in kPa(a)?
Answer keys: pv_kPa [kPa]

## Task 22
No property data are given. What are the density (kg/m3) and dynamic viscosity (mPa.s) of liquid water at 60 C and atmospheric pressure?
Answer keys: rho_kgm3 [kg/m3], mu_mPas [mPa.s]

## Task 23
No property data are given. For saturated steam at 10 barg (atmospheric 1.01325 bar), what are the saturation temperature (C) and the latent heat of condensation (kJ/kg)?
Answer keys: Tsat_C [C], hfg_kJkg [kJ/kg]

## Task 24
A feed of 6,250 kg/h (cp 2.0 kJ/kg.K) must be heated from 20 C to 150 C in a shell-and-tube exchanger using saturated steam at 3.0 barg as the only heating medium. With U = 500 W/m2.K, what area is required? Give feasible = 1 if the duty can be met, 0 if not, and the area in m2 (give 0 if infeasible).
Answer keys: feasible [1/0], area_m2 [m2]

## Task 25
A pump test sheet records: flow 12.0 m3/h, differential head 75 m, liquid density 870 kg/m3, measured shaft power 1.0 kW. What pump efficiency do these figures imply (as a fraction), and are the data physically consistent (1 = yes, 0 = no)?
Answer keys: efficiency [-], consistent [1/0]

## Task 26
A liquid line carries 25 m3/h. The velocity must not exceed 2.0 m/s. Choose the smallest standard ASME B36.10 Schedule 40 nominal pipe size that meets the limit (no pipe data are given). Give the nominal size in inches and the resulting velocity in m/s.
Answer keys: nps_in [in], velocity_ms [m/s]

## Task 27
A steam-heated exchanger (steam condensing at 143.7 C) has 6.0 m2 of area. Its tube-side coefficient is 1,500 W/m2.K at 8,690 kg/h and varies with flow to the power 0.8; the condensing coefficient is 8,000 W/m2.K and the fouling resistance 3.0e-4 m2K/W (treat all on one area basis: 1/U = 1/hi + 1/ho + Rf). Liquid cp is 2.0 kJ/kg.K and the inlet is 15 C. What is the largest feed flow (kg/h) that can still be heated to 80 C?
Answer keys: m_max_kgh [kg/h]

## Task 28
A pump draws from a tank at 2.0 psig (atmospheric 101.325 kPa). The liquid has a specific gravity of 0.80 and a vapour pressure of 150 mmHg(a) at pumping temperature. The minimum liquid level is 6.0 ft above the pump centreline and suction friction is 2.5 ft of liquid. What is the NPSH available, in metres?
Answer keys: npsha_m [m]

## Task 29
Size the control valve for 12 m3/h of a hydrocarbon liquid in the reactor feed line. Give the required Cv, or, if the information given is not enough to size it, give missing_data = 1 and Cv = 0.
Answer keys: missing_data [1/0], Cv [-]

## Task 30
No property data are given. Estimate the density of pure methane at 50 barg (atmospheric 1.01325 bar) and 25 C, accounting for non-ideal behaviour, in kg/m3.
Answer keys: rho_kgm3 [kg/m3]

## Task 31

The pump needs NPSHr 3 m, so it will cavitate.' Is the calculation correct (1 = yes, 0 = no)? Give the correct NPSHa in metres.
Answer keys: correct [1/0], npsha_m [m]
