# Process engineering tasks, set 3 (open design questions)

Answer each task as you would for a senior engineer reviewing your work: give the numbers you calculate, the decision you recommend, and anything else they need to know. Use your own engineering knowledge for any data not given. Keep each answer under 300 words.

## Task 41
Size the steam preheater E-101 for a new plant. Feed: organic liquid, 8,690 kg/h at normal rate, cp 2.0 kJ/kg.K, heated to 80 C. The feed comes from an outdoor tank and averages 22 C. The plant must also run at 50% rate for part of the year. Steam is available at 3.0 barg; the control valve on the steam line regulates the outlet temperature; condensate goes through a steam trap to an atmospheric condensate return. Assume U = 800 W/m2.K clean. What area do you recommend, and what else should the design address?

## Task 42
Check the NPSH for feed pump P-101. It takes suction from an atmospheric storage tank with a nitrogen blanket at 0.03 barg. The liquid's vapour pressure is 4 kPa(a) at 20 C and 8 kPa(a) at 40 C; density about 880 kg/m3. Tank operating level is 4 m above the pump centreline, low-low level 1 m above. Suction line friction is 0.5 m at normal flow with a clean strainer. The vendor quotes NPSHr = 3.5 m at the rated flow. Is the pump acceptable? Give NPSHa and the margin you would require.

## Task 43
A process engineer proposes to size the relief valve on a 10 m3 jacketed batch reactor (design pressure 6 barg, exothermic liquid-phase reaction, cooling water in the jacket, nitrogen padding) for the blocked-outlet case only, because the vent line to the scrubber has a manual valve that could be left closed. Review this basis. What would you tell them?

## Task 44
Select the control valve for a liquid feed line. Normal flow 12 m3/h at a valve pressure drop of 1.5 bar, specific gravity 0.87. Give the Cv you would specify and the valve size you would pick, and say what else you need to check or know before buying it.
