# Rubric for set 3 (written before any answers were seen). One point per item; 5 items per task.
41 (preheater): a) clean area at the normal case within +-15% of 3.9 m2; b) designs for a cold-feed (winter) and/or maximum-flow case, not the average; c) adds an explicit fouling allowance (fouling resistance or margin); d) identifies condensate stall / sub-atmospheric shell at low load or with oversizing, and proposes a remedy (pump-trap, vacuum breaker, condensate-side control or similar); e) flags missing data or design levers: fouling tendency/data, tube velocity, steam valve pressure drop.
42 (NPSH): a) uses the 40 C vapour pressure; b) uses the low-low level; c) allows for a dirty strainer or higher flow friction; d) NPSHa for 40 C and low-low level = 11.7 m +-5% (before any strainer allowance); e) states a margin requirement (ratio or metres, e.g. ANSI/HI 9.6.1) and/or checks NPSHr at maximum/runout flow.
43 (relief basis): a) rejects blocked outlet as the sole basis; b) names runaway reaction / loss of cooling as a credible, likely governing case needing reaction data (calorimetry, DIERS); c) names fire exposure; d) flags two-phase venting; e) names at least one further case or control: nitrogen regulator failure, jacket/tube leak, utility failure, or car-sealed-open management of the manual valve.
44 (control valve): a) Cv about 10.6 (+-3%) at normal conditions; b) asks for or uses maximum and minimum flows; c) checks valve opening/rangeability at max and min; d) checks choked flow, cavitation or flashing (vapour pressure, FL); e) picks a specific body size and states a characteristic or the installed-characteristic/pressure-drop variation.
