Overview

Build a team that fixes problems for good.

Lean removes waste from your processes. Six Sigma reduces the variation that causes defects, rework and delays. Together they give your people a shared, practical method for improving how work gets done. We teach it in-house, using your processes, your data and your problems, so every training day connects to the work your team does tomorrow.

Signs your team is ready

  • The same problems keep coming back after quick fixes
  • Rework, errors or customer complaints are eating into margin
  • Improvement depends on one or two people who are always busy
  • Teams argue about causes because the data isn't clear
  • You want a common improvement language across departments

The toolkit

Every tool is practised on your own processes and data, not textbook cases.

SIPOC, swim lane maps and value stream maps show how work really flows and where time is lost.

The eight wastes

Typical training projects

What a training project looks like.

Illustrative examples of projects participants lead during their training.

Invoice errors in finance

Before: Many invoices needed correction, delaying payment and tying up staff in follow-up.

After: A Green Belt team traced most errors to order entry, added a simple checklist and system prompts, and corrections dropped sharply.

Changeover time on a packing line

Before: Long, inconsistent changeovers pushed the line toward large batches and late orders.

After: Using SMED, the team moved setup work offline and standardized settings, freeing capacity for smaller, more frequent runs.

Customer call-backs in service

Before: Customers often had to call twice because their first request wasn't resolved.

After: Root cause analysis showed missing information at intake; a short script and knowledge base raised first-contact resolution.

Method

Lean removes waste. Six Sigma reduces variation.

Lean makes processes faster by removing steps that add no value. Six Sigma makes them more reliable by finding and fixing the causes of defects. Our training combines both, so your team can choose the right tool for each problem, from a quick waste walk to a data-driven DMAIC project.

Learning by doing

Your people learn by improving real work

We don't teach from generic case studies alone. Participants map their own processes, analyze their own data and lead projects their managers care about, with coaching at every phase. That is how new skills stick, and how the training starts paying back while it is still running.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do participants need a statistics background?

No. White and Yellow Belt need no statistics at all. Green and Black Belt introduce the statistics step by step, using your own data and simple tools.

How are improvement projects chosen?

We work with your leaders to pick projects that matter to the business, have a clear owner and can show measurable results within the training period.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Participants who complete their training receive a certificate of completion from Chmltech. If you need certification from an external body, we can help you plan for it.

What is the difference between the belt levels?

White and Yellow Belts build awareness and support projects. Green Belts lead projects in their own area. Black Belts lead larger, cross-functional projects and coach others.

Can we combine training with a process improvement project?

Yes. Many clients train a team while we work alongside them on a priority process, so learning and results happen together.
